Economic Concepts for Exams

Fiscal Deficit vs Revenue Deficit

Fiscal Deficit: Difference between government's total expenditure and total receipts (excluding borrowing). Current target: 5.1% of GDP for FY25.

Revenue Deficit: Difference between revenue expenditure and revenue receipts. Indicates government is borrowing to meet regular expenses.

Important for Prelims & Mains

Monetary Policy Tools

  • Repo Rate: Rate at which RBI lends to banks
  • Reverse Repo: Rate at which RBI borrows from banks
  • CRR: Cash Reserve Ratio
  • SLR: Statutory Liquidity Ratio
  • OMO: Open Market Operations
  • MSF: Marginal Standing Facility
