Sports Policies & Schemes

Khelo India Scheme

Objective: Revitalize sports culture at grassroot level through talent identification and development.

Components: Khelo India Youth Games, Khelo India University Games, Talent Search, Sports Infrastructure.

Budget: ₹3,397 crore (2023-24)

GS Paper II

Target Olympic Podium Scheme (TOPS)

Objective: Provide financial assistance and support to Olympic potential athletes.

Eligibility: Athletes ranked in top 20 globally or showing potential for Olympic medals.

Support: Monthly allowance, foreign training, coaching, equipment, medical insurance.

National Sports Development Code

Key Provisions: Age and tenure limits for sports federation officials, athlete representation in decision making, transparency in elections.

Recent Amendment: Mandatory compliance with National Anti-Doping Agency (NADA) rules.
