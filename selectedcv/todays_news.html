RBI Keeps Repo Rate Unchanged at 6.5%

The Monetary Policy Committee (MPC) of Reserve Bank of India decided to keep the policy repo rate unchanged at 6.5% for the seventh consecutive time.

Economic Indicators:

  • Inflation projection for FY25: 4.5%
  • GDP growth projection for FY25: 7%
  • Standing Deposit Facility rate: 6.25%

ISRO's RLV Technology Demonstrator Success

ISRO successfully conducted the third landing experiment of the Reusable Launch Vehicle (RLV) at Aeronautical Test Range, Chitradurga.

Significance:

  • Paves way for fully reusable launch vehicles
  • Reduces satellite launch costs by 80%
  • Demonstrates autonomous landing capability
