Important Concepts for Exams

Quantum Computing vs Classical Computing

Classical Computing: Uses bits (0 or 1). Sequential processing. Limited for complex simulations.

Quantum Computing: Uses qubits (0, 1, or both simultaneously - superposition). Parallel processing. Superior for cryptography, drug discovery, optimization problems.

What: Molecular scissors that can cut DNA at specific locations.

Applications: Treat genetic disorders, develop disease-resistant crops, eliminate disease-carrying mosquitoes.

Li-Fi Technology

Full Form: Light Fidelity

How it works: Uses visible light communication (LED bulbs) for data transmission.

Advantages over Wi-Fi: Faster (up to 100 Gbps), more secure (light doesn't penetrate walls), no electromagnetic interference.

Applications: Hospitals, aircraft, underwater communication.
