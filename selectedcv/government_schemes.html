Flagship

Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana (PMAY)

Launched: 2015 MoHUA

Housing for All by 2022 mission with components for urban and rural areas. Focus on affordable housing with credit linked subsidy.

Target Beneficiaries: Economically Weaker Sections
Budget (2023-24): ₹79,590 Cr
Status: Ongoing (Extended)
UPSC GS Paper II Prelims Expected Mains Answer Writing
New Launch

PM Kisan Samman Nidhi

Launched: 2019 Agriculture

Income support scheme providing ₹6,000 per year to farmer families in three equal installments through DBT.

Coverage: All landholding farmers
Beneficiaries: 11.5 Cr+ farmers
Budget (2023-24): ₹60,000 Cr
UPSC GS Paper III Prelims Important Rural Development
Health

Ayushman Bharat Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana (PM-JAY)

Launched: 2018 Health

World's largest health insurance scheme providing coverage of ₹5 lakh per family per year for secondary and tertiary care hospitalization.

Target: 10.74 Cr families
Hospitals Empaneled: 26,000+
Beneficiaries: 30 Cr+ people
UPSC GS Paper II Mains Important Social Justice
