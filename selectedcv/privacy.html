Quick Guidance for Students & Parents

Start career awareness early (8-10 years): aptitude tests, internships, club activities
Choose 11th stream based on interest + research (options can sometimes be changed in 12th)
Plan 1-2 years ahead for competitive exams (JEE/NEET/CLAT/CA/CUET)
Vocational/diploma routes lead to employment faster and allow further study later
Develop soft skills, internships, projects, coding & communication for better placements
For mid-stream changes, consult school/board for subject eligibility & catch-up plans
Consider hybrid options (Data Science + Biology, Bio-informatics, EdTech)
Parents: support exploration, not pressure — career counseling helps significantly
