Privacy Policy

Last Updated:

Welcome to SelectedCV! We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about how we handle your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your data.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Personal Information

When you use SelectedCV, we may collect:

  • Account Information: Name, email address, profile photo
  • CV Content: Professional information, work experience, education, skills, achievements
  • Contact Details: Phone numbers, email addresses, physical addresses you include in your CV

1.2 Technical Information

Automatically collected data:

  • Device Information: Device model, OS version, unique device identifiers
  • Usage Data: Feature usage, session duration, templates used
  • Performance Data: Crash reports, error logs, performance metrics

1.3 Authentication Data

  • Google account information (when using Google Sign-In)
  • Firebase Authentication tokens and identifiers

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • To provide and maintain the SelectedCV service
  • To create, store, and manage your professional resumes
  • To process subscription payments and manage premium features
  • To improve application performance and user experience
  • To send important notifications about service updates
  • To prevent fraud, abuse, and ensure application security
  • To comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes.

3. Data Storage and Security

3.1 Cloud Storage

Your CV data is stored securely in Google Firebase Cloud services. We implement industry-standard security measures including:

  • Data encryption in transit and at rest
  • Secure authentication protocols
  • Regular security assessments
  • Access controls and monitoring

3.2 Data Loss Disclaimer

While we implement reasonable security measures, we cannot guarantee absolute data protection. We are not responsible for data loss due to:

  • Technical failures, bugs, or system errors
  • User error or accidental deletion
  • Third-party service outages or interruptions
  • Security breaches beyond our reasonable control
  • Force majeure events including natural disasters

Users are strongly advised to maintain their own backups of important CV data and export important resumes regularly.

3.3 Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. You can request account deletion at any time, which will permanently remove your data from our systems.

4. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services that may process your data:

4.1 Google Services

  • Firebase: Authentication, database storage, analytics
  • Google Play Store: In-app purchases and subscription management
  • AdMob: Advertisement services (free version only)

4.2 Third-Party Policies

These services have their own privacy policies and terms of service. We recommend reviewing them:

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Firebase Terms: https://firebase.google.com/terms
  • Google Play Terms: https://play.google.com/about/play-terms

5. Payment and Subscription Information

5.1 Payment Processing

All payments are processed through Google Play Store. We do not store or have access to your complete payment information.

5.2 No Refund Policy

ALL PURCHASES ARE FINAL AND NON-REFUNDABLE. We do not provide refunds for:

  • Subscription payments (monthly or yearly)
  • Accidental purchases
  • Unused subscription periods
  • Service dissatisfaction
  • Technical issues that are subsequently resolved

You may cancel your subscription at any time through Google Play Store settings, but no refunds will be provided for the current billing period.

6. Your Rights and Choices

You have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access: View and download your CV data
  • Correction: Update or correct your personal information
  • Deletion: Delete your account and associated data
  • Export: Export your resumes in PDF format
  • Opt-out: Opt-out of promotional communications
  • Withdraw Consent: Withdraw previously given consent

To exercise these rights, contact us at support@selectedcv.com

7. Children's Privacy

SelectedCV is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have collected information from a child under 13, please contact us immediately.

8. International Data Transfers

Your data may be processed on servers located outside of your country. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place for international data transfers.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify users of material changes through the application or via email. Continued use of the Service after changes constitutes acceptance of the modified Privacy Policy.

10. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact us:

  • Email: support@selectedcv.com
  • Response Time: 2-3 business days
  • Data Protection Officer: privacy@selectedcv.com
